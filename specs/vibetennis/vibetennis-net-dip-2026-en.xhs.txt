Why is the net lower in the middle? Not sagging. Pulled down.

1.07 m at the posts, 0.914 m at the centre: 15.6 cm lower.

Is it just slack? A rope hanging between two points makes a catenary, solved in 1691. But the net is not a catenary.

The cord is tensioned to 1.8–2.0 kN (ITF guidance). With the centre strap loose, the middle sits near 99 cm. Pull the strap down: 91.4 cm. A point pulled down turns both sides into straight lines: a shallow V.

History: at the first Wimbledon in 1877 the posts were 5 ft and the centre 3 ft 3 in. By 1882 the posts were 3 ft 6 in and the centre 3 ft, as today.

Crosscourt is safer: the diagonal is 25.15 m, 1.38 m longer, and it crosses the lowest part of the net. In a simple model at 100 km/h, crosscourt clears by about 45 cm versus about 30 cm down the line. Pros agree: from the backhand corner, 52.9% crosscourt, 17.4% down the line.
