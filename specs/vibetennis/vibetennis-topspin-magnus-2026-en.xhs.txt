Why does topspin suddenly dive?

A 108 km/h forehand clears the net by 1.6 m, looks sure to go long, then dips in 1 m inside the baseline (model estimate).

The Magnus effect: with topspin, air on the bottom of the ball clings longer and is flung upward, so the reaction pushes the ball down. At 108 km/h and 3200 rpm that force is about 0.41 N, against a 0.56 N weight. Gravity, boosted 1.7×.

Same 108 km/h and 15.2° launch: no spin flies 32.1 m, out; 4000 rpm lands at 22.5 m, in.

Over 70% of the deviation happens in the second half. It never suddenly drops. You only see it late.

"Model estimate" figures come from Cross's published model, not measurements.
