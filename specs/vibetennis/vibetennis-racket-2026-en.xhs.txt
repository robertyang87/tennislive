Did the racket change tennis?

For nearly a century, wooden rackets had about 70 sq in of strings. 1963: Lacoste's steel patent (Wilson T2000). 1976: Howard Head's oversize patent. c. 1980: carbon fibre.

Head stretched the face toward the handle to bring the "no-jar" point into the middle, where people actually hit.

The "sweet spot" is three points: fastest rebound, vibration node, centre of percussion. The best feel is usually the node.

A wider head twists less on a miss-hit: the ball barely changes direction but comes off slower, and in a real swing flies higher and longer.

Stiffer frames mostly help miss-hits and serves. 10% less swing weight buys only about 3% more swing speed.

ITF: bigger heads control off-centre hits better; stiffer frames lose less energy. But balls changed too, and no study isolates the racket.
