Did the strings change tennis?

1874: sheep gut. Then nylon. 1997: Kuerten wins Roland-Garros on polyester. Later: hybrids.

Strings change three things. Power (restitution): every string returns ~95% of the energy; looser strings add only ~3% groundstroke speed. Spin (Coulomb friction): the ball rolls before it leaves the strings, so more grip adds nothing; spin comes from mains sliding and snapping back. The 1977 spaghetti racket doubled that and was banned in 1978. Tension (stress relaxation): ~30% lost right after stringing, fastest for poly, so pros swap rackets at every ball change.

Serve-and-volley fell from ~40% of points (1990s) to under 10% after 2005 (Match Charting Project, aces excluded). Topspin returns dip at the volleyer's feet; in 2010-18 it won 66.8% of points vs 72.2% staying back (Sackmann). Rackets changed too; no study isolates the strings.
