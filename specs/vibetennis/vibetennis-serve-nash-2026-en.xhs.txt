Serve: which side?

A first serve lands in under half a second, so the returner has to guess. Serve one side every time and you get read.

Game theory's answer: mix. von Neumann (1928) and Nash (1950) showed there is a balance neither side can exploit; von Neumann's own example was rock-paper-scissors.

Mixing isn't 50–50. On the ad side Sinner serves wide 58.7% and down the T 35.4%, yet wins 69.7% vs 71.1%: no real difference.

Our test on 2,151 men's matches since 2023 (Match Charting Project): wide 65.0%, T 64.7%. Only 5.3% of 1,056 groups deviate, about what chance gives. Big points too.

But a 2025 study that adds the score finds most top players aren't quite there.

The best serve is one even you don't see coming.

Do you keep throwing the same thing in rock-paper-scissors?
