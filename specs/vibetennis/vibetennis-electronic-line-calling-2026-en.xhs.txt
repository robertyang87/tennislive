Electronic line calling: how accurate is it?

The oval you see in a replay isn't filmed — it's calculated. Hawk-Eye's cameras each find the ball's centre, their lines of sight meet in 3D, and the points become a trajectory.

At 340 fps a 200 km/h ball moves about 16 cm between frames, but touches the ground for only about 5 ms. The footprint is modelled, never measured.

Line judges: about 2 cm uncertainty; 39% of challenged calls were wrong. ITF top certification: 100% correct beyond 5 mm.

Not infallible: within 5 mm is permitted error.
