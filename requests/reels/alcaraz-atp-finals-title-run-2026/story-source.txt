Carlos Alcaraz's Nitto ATP Finals champion story
22 November 2026
By Sam Jacot
© Corinne Dubreuil/ATP Tour
Share:
Look back at highlights, photos and best stories from Alcaraz's title-winning week in Turin
Carlos Alcaraz ended his season in style at the Nitto ATP Finals, where he defeated Alexander Zverev in the final to lift the trophy for the first time.
Look back at Alcaraz’s highlights, best photos and stories from his standout week in northern Italy, where he went 5-0 to capture his sixth trophy of the season and 30th overall.
Before the action began, Alcaraz walked the Blue Carpet in T
urin's Piazza San Carlo
with the other seven singles finalists. Fans lined the streets to give their support to Alcaraz and the stars, before all eight posed for the official photo.
Photo Credit: Jack Howell/ATP Tour
Three days later and the Spaniard began his campaign on court, when he dropped just four games in a ruthless display against Frances Tiafoe, triumphing 6-2, 6-2.
Alcaraz then overcame Daniil Medvedev 6-2, 6-4 to move to 2-0 in round-robin play and survived a scare in that match, receiving treatment for a left leg issue. With his win, Alcaraz advanced to the semi-finals.
The Spaniard returned to court against Flavio Cobolli and moved freely, despite the heavy strapping on his left leg. Alcaraz did not face a break point in the clash and struck 29 winners to triumph 6-4, 6-1.
“Today's match, for me, was an important match because we were testing myself,” Alcaraz said after his win against the Italian. “We were testing how I was going to feel my body, how I was going to react after yesterday's treatment, after the day of yesterday, and I just felt well.
“I try not to go too hard with myself physically, which I just tried to play really hard, really strong, and aggressive, and I think I could do it. So I'm just happy about it. I'm looking forward to tomorrow.”
Into the semi-finals, Alcaraz found a way past Ben Shelton in a thriller to return to the title match in Turin. The Spaniard recovered from a slow start, producing his best level in the third-set tie-break to record a 3-6, 6-3, 7-6(1) win and improve to 5-1 in his Lexus ATP Head2Head series with Shelton.

Alcaraz then capped his impressive week with a first-class display against Zverev, becoming just the third Spaniard to win the title at the prestigious year-end event. Alcaraz recorded a 6-2, 7-6(7) victory, saving three set points in the second-set tie-break.
"For me it's been an amazing week," Alcaraz said."I couldn't have ended the week in a better way. I'm just really proud and really happy with everything I have done this week. I've been through really difficult moments within myself, with my team, and we went through everything playing great tennis. This trophy is something I have been chasing for a really long time. It means the world to me."
