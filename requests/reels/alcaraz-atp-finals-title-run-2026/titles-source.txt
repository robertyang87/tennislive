Alcaraz ties Sinner in Big Titles battle with Nitto ATP Finals victory
22 November 2026
By ATP Staff
© ATP Tour/Getty Images
Share:
Alcaraz defeats Zverev for first Nitto ATP Finals triumph
Carlos Alcaraz has levelled his Big Titles battle with Jannik Sinner at 17 titles apiece by winning the Nitto ATP Finals for the first time.
The Spaniard defeated two-time season finale winner Alexander Zverev on Sunday for the trophy, completing an undefeated run through the year-end championships in Italy.
Alcaraz now owns 17 Big Titles – a combination of Grand Slam championships, trophies at the Nitto ATP Finals and ATP Masters 1000 tournaments, and Olympic singles gold medals — after claiming the final two Big Titles of the season, in Paris and Turin.
The 23-year-old has now claimed all but one type of Big Title: an Olympic singles gold medal. He has won seven major crowns, the Nitto ATP Finals trophy and nine ATP Masters 1000 titles.
This was Alcaraz's third Big Title of 2026, having also triumphed at the Australian Open to
complete the Career Grand Slam

Alcaraz had competed in the Nitto ATP Finals on three previous occasions, falling in last year's championship match to Sinner. He became the first Spaniard to emerge victorious at the season finale since Alex Corretja in 1998.
The Lexus ATP Head2Head rivalry between Alcaraz and Sinner now has another twist — Both men own a 30-9 record in tour-level finals, according to the Infosys ATP Win/Loss Index.
The No. 3 player in the PIF ATP Rankings went 6-1 in finals this season, including 3-1 with a Big Title on the line.
Alcaraz has won a Big Title for every 3.7 tournaments he has played. Only Novak Djokovic (3.3) and Rafael Nadal (3.5) are ahead of him in the category.
