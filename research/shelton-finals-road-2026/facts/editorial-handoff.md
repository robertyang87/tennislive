## 使用边界

- TE dates explicitly European listing dates, not uniformly local match dates. Selected eight nodes have verified local dates.
- 17 genuine official PTL tournament draw PDFs archived. Four Grand Slam PTL endpoints returned identical placeholder PDFs, rejected as evidence; GS official article reports and TE actual results used instead.
- All-match CSV is secondary-source extraction; selected four title final scores and late autumn matches were cross-checked against genuine official draw text, GS terminal matches against official GS articles.
- ATP main website returned403 to raw HTTP on several articles; preserve this in article-manifest, do not claim raw article archive exists when absent. Search-service article access was used for claims.
- No 2026 singles retirement or walkover in extracted62records. Do not invent injury narrative.
- Full/all-time superlative records are omitted from safe narration pending two independent corroborating sources.
