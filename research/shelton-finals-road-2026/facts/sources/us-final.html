Who won the 2026 US Open?

Saturday, 12 September 2026 08:33 PM EDT

Who won the 2026 US Open men's singles title?

Alexander Zverev won the 2026 US Open men's singles title on Sunday, defeating American Ben Shelton, 6-3, 7-6(2), 5-7, 6-2, for his second major title.

Who won the 2026 US Open women's singles title?

Elena Rybakina won the 2026 US Open women's singles title on Saturday, defeating two-time defending champion Aryna Sabalenka, 6-4, 5-7, 6-2. 

Who won the 2026 US Open men's doubles title?

Christian Harrison and Neal Skupski won the 2026 US Open men's doubles title, defeating 2024 finalists Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz, 6-4, 7-6(6).

Who won the 2026 US Open women's doubles title?

Taylor Townsend and Katerina Siniakova won the 2026 US Open women's doubles title, defeating the 2021 girls' doubles champs, Ashlyn Krueger and Robin Montgomery, 5-7, 6-0, 6-2. The No. 1 seeds, Townsend and Siniakova completed the career Grand Slam together as a team, Siniakova's second time earning the honor, after earning the career Golden Slam with Barbora Krejcikova.

Who won the 2026 US Open mixed doubles title?

Karolina Muchova and Jakub Mensik, both of Czechia, won the 2026 US Open mixed doubles title, defeating Belinda Bencic and Flavio Cobolli in the final, 6-3, 1-6, [10-6]. 

Who won the 2026 US Open boys' singles title?

Marcel Latak won the 2026 US Open boys' singles title, upsetting top seed Guto Miguel, 3-6, 7-5, 7-6(13). Latak became the first American boy to win the singles title in Flushing Meadows since Taylor Fritz in 2015.

Who won the 2026 US Open girls' singles title?

Top seed Sun Xinran won the 2026 US Open girls' singles title, defeating Anna Pushkareva, 6-4, 4-6, 6-0.

Who won the 2026 US Open boys' doubles title?

Aaron Gabet and Valentin Gonzalez-Galino won the 2026 US Open boys' doubles title, defeating the all-American duo of Marcel Latak and Tanishk Konduri, 7-6(5), 2-6, [11-9].

Who won the 2026 US Open girls' doubles title?

Annika Penickova and Kristina Penickova won the 2026 US Open girls' doubles title, defeating Polina Berezina and Alisa Terentyeva, 7-6(11), 6-3.

Who won the 2026 US Open men's wheelchair singles title?

Alfie Hewett won the 2026 US Open men's wheelchair singles title, upsetting world No. 1 and defending champion Tokito Oda, 6-0, 7-6(5), stopping Oda from claiming the calendar-year Grand Slam.

Who won the 2026 US Open men's wheelchair doubles title?

Top seeds Alfie Hewett and Gordon Reid won the 2026 US Open men's wheelchair doubles title, defeating No. 2 seeds Martin De la Puente and Ruben Spaargaren, 2-6, 6-3, [10-7].

Who won the 2026 US Open women's wheelchair singles title?

Yui Kamiji won the 2026 US Open women's wheelchair singles title, defeating Diede De Groot, 6-2, 1-6, 6-3.

Who won the 2026 US Open women's wheelchair doubles title?

Yui Kamiji and Zhu Zhenzhen won the 2026 US Open women's wheelchair doubles title, defeating Guo Luoyao and Momoko Ohtani, 6-1, 6-2.

Who won the 2026 US Open quad wheelchair singles title?

Niels Vink won the 2026 US Open quad wheelchair singles title, defeating Sam Schroder, 6-2, 7-5. Vink claimed the calendar-year Grand Slam as a result of winning the tournament.

Who won the 2026 US Open quad wheelchair doubles title?

Sam Schroder and Jin Woodman won the 2026 US Open quad wheelchair doubles title, defeating top seeds Guy Sasson and Niels Vink, 6-2, 2-6, [10-7].

Who won the 2026 US Open boys' wheelchair singles title?

Matthew Knoesen won the 2026 US Open boys' wheelchair singles title, defeating Sonny Rennison, 6-0, 6-2.

Who won the 2026 US Open girls' wheelchair singles title?

Seira Matsuoka won the 2026 US Open girls' wheelchair singles title, defeating Luna Gryp, 7-6(3), 6-3.

Who won the 2026 US Open boys' wheelchair doubles title?

Matthew Knoesen and Hirofumi Inoue won the 2026 US Open boys' wheelchair doubles title, defeating Will Barton and Lorenzo Politano, 6-1, 6-0.

Who won the 2026 US Open girls' wheelchair doubles title?

Paula Michelle Lopez Meza and Seira Matsuoka won the 2026 US Open girls' wheelchair doubles title, defeating Emma Gjerseth and Lucy Heald, 6-4, 6-3.
