Frances Tiafoe dazzles, downs Daniil Medvedev for 2026 US Open QF spot

Adesina O. Koiki / 
Sunday, 6 September 2026 06:37 PM EDT

WHAT HAPPENED

No. 11 seed Frances Tiafoe defeated seventh-seeded Daniil Medvedev in straight sets, 7-6 (1), 6-4, 7-6 (6), inside Louis Armstrong Stadium to advance to the quarterfinals of the 2026 US Open.

The new chant catching on amongst fans at the 2026 US Open is “One, Two, Three, Foe!” After today, it’s now one, two, three, four straight wins for Tiafoe in Flushing, with the latest one probably the most impressive.

Tiafoe’s Fortress of Solitude once again turned the American into Superman at the US Open, as the No. 11 seed turned back the challenge of 2021 champion Medvedev with a 7-6 (1), 6-4, 7-6 (6), victory inside Louis Armstrong Stadium and advanced to the quarterfinals at the 2026 US Open. Tiafoe also bucked history between the two, as Medvedev had won six of the seven prior meetings before the tables were turned in dramatic fashion on the eve of Labor Day.

Photos: Medvedev vs. Tiafoe, Round 4

The first set, a rollercoaster of a frame that lasted an hour and 14 minutes and featured extended baseline rallies, proved the difference maker. (The first game of the match alone took almost 10 minutes.) Medvedev, after winning three straight games, served for the set at 6-5 but was broken for the third time by Tiafoe to force a tiebreak.

Tiafoe was on fire in the breaker, winning every point on his serve on his way to winning the set while dropping just one point.

Medvedev’s opportunity to realistically get back in the match occurred in the third set, but a couple of shots from Tiafoe both stunted the seventh seed’s momentum and wowed the Armstrong crowd in the process. Serving at 2-3, 30-0, Tiafoe handled a dipping Medvedev return with a pirouetting backhand half-volley winner at the net to set up game point.

 

With Medvedev holding a set point on his serve, Tiafoe almost brought the open roof of Armstrong down with how he kept the set alive; approaching the net, the American stuck two consecutive volleys before sending a final shot at net almost through Medvedev’s legs for 6-6 in the tiebreak.

After another point won on Medvedev’s serve after the change of ends with a passing shot that Medvedev could only shank into the net, Tiafoe successfully brought the curtain down on his first match-point chance.

“More and more I can enjoy myself and have fun,” Tiafoe said. “It seems like I can bring my best stuff out, and that's kind of what that is. When you feel so comfortable and won so much on a court, you just have that extra little belief, like I feel like I can do anything on that court.”

 

We should have known that a different result from the previous matchups between the players was in the cards, not only because the match was in New York but, specifically, it took place inside Louis Armstrong Stadium, where Tiafoe is now a perfect 10-0 at the US Open.

“It’s the people’s court,” Tiafoe said about Armstrong in his on-court interview after the match.

It was a star-studded Armstrong that witnessed the encounter, with celebs including basketball Hall of Famer Lisa Leslie, 2026 No. 1 overall NBA Draft pick A.J. Dybantsa and Emmy-nominated actress/comedian Yvonne Orji.

WHAT IT MEANS

Tiafoe won as many sets against Medvedev today (three) as he did in the 18 sets between the two in their career head-to-head matchup.

After a blip in the radar screen in the form of a third-round loss against Jan-Lennard Struff last year, Tiafoe is officially back in the business of making deep runs at the US Open. Tiafoe made the fourth round of the US Open for five consecutive years, from 2020-24, with two runs to the semifinals in the process (2022, 2024).

Similar to 2023, when Tiafoe’s run was stopped by Ben Shelton, another Yankee will await Tiafoe in the quarterfinals of the US Open; Alex Michelsen defeated Tomas Martin Etcheverry to set up an All-American affair on Labor Day for a semifinal spot.

MATCH POINT 

The win extends Tiafoe’s winning streak against Top 10 opponents to five, a stretch that includes wins against Flavio Cobolli, Felix Auger-Aliassime and Taylor Fritz en route to the Halle title, and another victory against Auger-Aliassime en route to the ATP Masters 1000 Cincinnati final. Only Tiafoe and Jannik Sinner (10) have at least five wins against Top 10 opponents in 2026.

Tiafoe and Medvedev are the two iron men of Grand Slam tennis, as they currently own the two longest consecutive Grand Slam appearances streak. Tiafoe’s 2026 US Open marked his 40th straight Grand Slam played, while Medvedev is right behind him with 38 straight major appearances. (Medvedev’s ban from participating at Wimbledon in 2022 is not counted as part of the streak.)
