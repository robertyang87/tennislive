Daniil Medvedev Crowned Almaty Open ATP 250 Champion
Daniil Medvedev claimed the singles title at the ATP 250 Almaty Open, held from October 17 to 25 at Almaty Arena.
The World No. 5 and top seed defeated Georgia's Nikoloz Basilashvili, ranked No. 128 in the ATP Rankings, in a three-set final, 6-7(6), 6-1, 6-3.
The championship match lasted one hour and 53 minutes, with the opening set proving particularly competitive. Basilashvili challenged the tournament favourite and edged the first-set tiebreak 8-6 to take an early lead.
However, Medvedev responded strongly in the second set, taking complete control of the match. The Russian improved his return game, applied consistent pressure on Basilashvili's serve, and comfortably claimed the set 6-1.
In the deciding set, Medvedev maintained his momentum, secured another crucial break of serve, and closed out the match 6-3 to seal the championship.
Throughout the final, Medvedev fired 22 aces, committed four double faults, and converted four of his ten break-point opportunities. Basilashvili recorded three aces and three double faults. Medvedev also held a clear advantage in winners, hitting 26 compared to Basilashvili's 17.
Basilashvili's run to the final was particularly impressive, having entered the tournament through qualifying. The Georgian defeated Greece's Stefanos Tsitsipas in the semi-finals, while Medvedev advanced to the championship match after Kazakhstan's Alexander Bublik was forced to retire from their semi-final encounter due to a right knee injury.
The victory marked Medvedev's fourth ATP Tour title of the 2026 season. His triumph in Almaty added another trophy to his career collection and earned him 250 ATP ranking points.
With Medvedev's victory, the nine-day Almaty Open ATP 250 concluded its singles competition. Earlier, India's Yuki Bhambri and American Ryan Seggerman claimed the doubles title after defeating Austria's Lucas Miedler and Neil Oberleitner in the final.
