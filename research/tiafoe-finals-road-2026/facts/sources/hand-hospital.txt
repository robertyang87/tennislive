08 August 2026 | 09:25 AM America/New_York Frances Tiafoe Undergoes Hand Surgery Performed by HSS Surgeon Dr. Michelle Carlson Just days after a painful mid-match exit from the Canadian Open, tennis star Frances Tiafoe underwent right-hand surgery performed by Michelle G. Carlson, MD , Hand and Upper Extremity Surgeon at HSS, to remove a cyst that had been causing persistent problems. In a social media update to his Instagram story, Tiafoe credited Dr. Carlson and the HSS team for successfully diagnosing and treating the condition: “Big S/O to Dr. Michelle Carlson and the Hospital for Special Surgery team. They were able to diagnose and take out a cyst that has been causing some issues for some time now. Everything went well and I appreciate all of the messages and support.
