Frances Tiafoe Undergoes Hand Surgery Performed by HSS Surgeon Dr. Michelle Carlson

Just days after a painful mid-match exit from the Canadian Open, tennis star Frances Tiafoe underwent right-hand surgery performed by Michelle G. Carlson, MD, Hand and Upper Extremity Surgeon at HSS, to remove a cyst that had been causing persistent problems.

In a social media update to his Instagram story, Tiafoe credited Dr. Carlson and the HSS team for successfully diagnosing and treating the condition: “Big S/O to Dr. Michelle Carlson and the Hospital for Special Surgery team. They were able to diagnose and take out a cyst that has been causing some issues for some time now. Everything went well and I appreciate all of the messages and support. I'll be back soon.” 

Read the full article at tennisuptodate.com. The story can also be found on Yardbarker, Yahoo! Sports, MSN, motorcyclesports.net, Tennis Temple, Tennis Head, Tennis World USA, Daily Express, Pro Football & Sports Network and Essentially Sports.
