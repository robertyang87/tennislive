How and where to watch Roland-Garros 2026?

Check out the official broadcasters for the Parisian Grand Slam, taking place at Porte d’Auteuil from 18 May to 7 June!

Tuesday 12 May 2026

This year again, Roland-Garros will enjoy exceptional TV coverage in France, courtesy of France Télévisions and Prime Video, as well as abroad. The tournament will be broadcast on more than 170 television channels and digital platforms in 220 countries. Last year, the Parisian Major was the most-watched Grand Slam tournament, with more than 324 million viewers, showing its strong appeal.

For this new edition, the schedule for French viewers will be very clear:

- Opening Week and all the daytime matches (up to and including the quarter-finals will be broadcast exclusively on France Télévisions.

- Prime Video will provide subscribers with exclusive coverage of the feature match in the 11 evening sessions, including two quarter-finals.

- The semi-finals and finals will be co-broadcast by both French broadcasters.

France Télévisions will also broadcast Roland-Garros on its digital channel Sport throughout the three weeks of the tournament. France 4 will expand its coverage of Opening Week, showing at least two matches a day from 12:30pm on Monday 18 to Wednesday 20 May, and from 1:30pm on the last two days (Thursday 21 and Friday 22 May).

A look at what's new for the tournament!

Broad international exposure

Following a particularly successful first year, Warner Bros.Discovery is continuing its ambitious coverage of the Parisian Grand Slam in the US, including the day’s best matches on TNT, full coverage on HBO Max, a daily multiplex on TruTV and dedicated content for digital platforms Bleacher Report and House of Highlights. CNN will also be promoting the tournament in the US. Warner Bros.Discovery will have several TV studios at Roland-Garros, including one located in the heart of the stadium in the Jardin des Mousquetaires.

Roland-Garros will continue to be broadcast around the world, including in more than 50 countries in Europe on Eurosport, in China on CCTV, in South America and Latin America on ESPN, in the Middle East, North Africa and Southeast Asia on beIN Sports, in Japan on Wowow, in India on Sony and in Australia on Nine and Stan. The FFT is also pleased to welcome Tencent as the tournament’s new digital broadcaster in China for the next three editions, from 2026 to 2028.
