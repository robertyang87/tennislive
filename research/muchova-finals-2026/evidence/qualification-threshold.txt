One WTA Finals singles spot remains open: Here’s how Karolina Muchova can clinch it
previews
1m read
20 Oct 2026
1d ago
Wang Zhao/AFP via Getty Images
As the season enters its final stretch, one singles place remains up for grabs at the WTA Finals in Indian Wells. Seven players have qualified: Elena Rybakina, Aryna Sabalenka, Mirra Andreeva, Jessica Pegula, Coco Gauff, Elina Svitolina and Linda Noskova.
Three players remain in contention for the last spot: Karolina Muchova (No. 7, 4,735 points), Iga Swiatek (No. 9, 4,189) and Marta Kostyuk (No. 10, 3,920). Muchova is not playing this week, and Swiatek has withdrawn from Ningbo, leaving Kostyuk as the only player whose results can affect the race.
Secure your seats for the WTA Finals
After a season-long race, the world's top players converge in the California desert with the WTA's biggest title still to be won.
Buy your tickets
The scenario is straightforward: If Kostyuk does not reach the final, Muchova secures the last spot.
Kostyuk will open against Ekaterina Alexandrova on Wednesday.
The WTA Finals begin Nov. 8 in Indian Wells.
