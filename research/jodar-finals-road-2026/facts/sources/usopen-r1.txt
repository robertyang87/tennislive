Results and highlights Arthur Kapetanakis / Wednesday, 2 September 2026 06:01 PM EDT Ben Shelton , Jessica Pegula and Daniil Medvedev were among the winners on Wednesday at the 2026 US Open, while Bu Yunchaokete upset 12th seed Rafael Jodar . Aryna Sabalenka , Carlos Alcaraz , Frances Tiafoe all advanced in the evening session. Shelton survived the big-serving Hubert Hurkacz , 6-3, 5-7, 7-6(3), 7-5, then unleashed a wide-eyed celebration reminiscent of a New Zealand haka. "He hit another gear when I was up a set and a break, and the match [went from] fully in my control to up in the air," Shelton said of the former world No. 6's effort in Ashe. "I thought that I found so many things in terms of game style that I want to be playing towards the end of that match. That was a big one for me. "I think that's why the emotions came out at the end." Video: 2026 US Open Top Moments: Day 4 Pegula eased past Sofia Kenin , 6-3, 6-1, opening play in Ashe with a victory in the all-American showdown, while Medvedev knocked off former University of Texas star Sebastian Gorzny , 6-1, 6-3, 7-5, to reach the third round. Other notable results for Top 10 players included Karolina Muchova 's 6-1, 6-0 win against Katie Boulter and Alex de Minaur 's 6-2, 6-2, 6-4 victory against Andrea Guerrieri , with newlyweds Boulter and De Minaur experiencing contrasting fortunes. Video: 2026 US Open: Quotes from Day 4 What were the biggest upsets on Wednesday at the 2026 US Open? Chinese lucky loser Bu upset Jodar, 6-2, 6-1, 6-1, winning a delayed first-round match to knock out one of the in-form players of the summer hard-court swing. The fast-rising Jodar, 19, reached the Washington final and the Montreal semis. Despite this being his first US Open appearance, the former University of Virginia star was among the tournament's favorites amidst a breakout season. Video: Jodar vs. Bu | 2026 Round 1 Alex Michelsen knocked out 16th seed Brandon Nakashima , 6-3, 6-4, 6-4, in an all-American match in Grandstand, while Daniel Merida beat 23rd seed Andrey Rublev , 6-7(3), 6-2, 6-4, 6-4. Alex Michelsen, the draw disruptor Which Americans advanced on Wednesday at the 2026 US Open? Shelton, Pegula, Tiafoe and Michelsen were joined by Iva Jovic , Emma Navarro , Tommy Paul , Ann Li , Taylor Townsend , Zachary Svajda and Michael Zheng among the American winners on Day 4 of singles main-draw play. Jovic, Zheng and Svajda were all playing delayed opening-round matches. The 18-year-old Jovic advanced to the US Open second round for the third straight year with a 7-5, 6-3 victory against Magdalena Frech . She is seeded at her home major for the first time and could face good friend Alexandra Eala in Round 3.
