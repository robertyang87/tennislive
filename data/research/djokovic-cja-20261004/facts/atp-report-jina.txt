## Djokovic preserves his Beijing perfection! Serbian downs in-form Zverev for SF spot

Serbian sets last-four meeting with Medvedev at ATP 500 event

October 04, 2026

![Image 7: Novak Djokovic in action against Alexander Zverev on Sunday at the China Open.](https://www.atptour.com/-/media/images/news/2026/10/04/13/25/djokovic-beijing-2026-qf.jpg)

Greg Baker/AFP via Getty Images

Novak Djokovic in action against Alexander Zverev on Sunday at the China Open.By Andy West

[Novak Djokovic](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/novak-djokovic/d643/overview) delivered a statement victory against the ATP Tour’s in-form player to forge a new piece of tennis history on Sunday evening in Beijing.

The 39-year-old Serbian overcame top seed [Alexander Zverev](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/alexander-zverev/z355/overview) 4-6, 6-4, 6-4 in a high-quality encounter to keep his bid for a record-extending seventh [China Open](https://www.atptour.com/en/tournaments/beijing/747/overview) title intact. With his two-hour, 35-minute triumph, Djokovic improved to 32-0 in Beijing, the best start in men’s singles at a tour-level event in the Open Era: Djokovic was previously tied on that list with his great Lexus ATP Head2Head rival [Rafael Nadal](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/rafael-nadal/n409/overview), who won his first 31 matches at [Roland Garros](https://www.atptour.com/en/tournaments/roland-garros/520/overview).

### Top Videos

Djokovic delivered a steely performance to set a semi-final showdown with his fellow former World No. 1 [Daniil Medvedev](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/daniil-medvedev/mm58/overview). He dropped serve in the opening game of the match, a lapse that proved enough to cost him the first set, but he showcased trademark resilience and desire to haul himself back into the match before clinching victory with the only break of the decider.

With his second Top-2 victory of the season after his [Australian Open](https://www.atptour.com/en/tournaments/australian-open/580/overview) semi-final win against [Jannik Sinner](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/jannik-sinner/s0ag/overview), Djokovic improved to 10-5 in his Lexus ATP Head2Head series with Zverev. He is just the third man in PIF ATP Rankings history (since 1973) to earn a Top-3 win after turning 39 years of age, after [Ken Rosewall](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/ken-rosewall/r075/overview) and [Jimmy Connors](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/jimmy-connors/c044/overview).

Djokovic’s next assignment will be a semi-final meeting with Medvedev, who earlier overcame [Francisco Cerundolo](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/francisco-cerundolo/c0au/overview) 7-6(4), 6-3. Having arrived in Beijing after lifting an ATP 250 crown in Hangzhou, former World No. 1 Medvedev is now on a seven-match winning streak, but he will enter Monday's last-four clash with a 5-10 deficit in the pair's Lexus ATP Head2Head series.

[Alex de Minaur](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/alex-de-minaur/dh58/overview) and [Hubert Hurkacz](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/hubert-hurkacz/hb71/overview) will contest the semi-final in the bottom half of the draw. The fifth-seeded De Minaur downed [Andrey Rublev](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/andrey-rublev/re44/overview) 1-6, 6-2, 6-3 to equal his semi-final run in Beijing from a year ago. By reaching the last four in the Chinese capital, De Minaur has risen four spots to ninth in the PIF ATP Live Race To Turin, boosting his hopes of a third consecutive qualification for the [Nitto ATP Finals](https://www.atptour.com/en/tournaments/nitto-atp-finals/605/overview).

Meanwhile Hurkacz continued his resurgence on the hard courts of China after injury struggles. The Chengdu finalist ousted [Karen Khachanov](https://www.atptour.com/en/players/karen-khachanov/ke29/overview) 7-6(2), 6-2 for his first semi-final above ATP 250 level since Rotterdam in February 2025.
