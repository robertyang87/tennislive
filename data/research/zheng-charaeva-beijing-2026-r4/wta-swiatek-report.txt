Swiatek dominated the opening set, winning the first five games and earning two set points in the sixth game before Jovic held serve to get on the scoreboard. The Pole then held serve to close out the set on her fifth set point. The second set began with Swiatek breaking early to take a 2-0 lead, but Jovic began finding more range and depth on her groundstrokes. The 18-year-old, ranked a career-high No. 13 in the world, broke back to level the set. Jovic, who entered the match on a six-match winning streak after winning the Guadalajara title last month, saved two more break points in the next game to take a 3-2 lead. But Swiatek broke twice in a row to complete the victory in 1 hour, 36 minutes. "For sure (today), I was using more of the chances that I got," Swiatek told the media after the win. "I was more proactive. Maybe changing the directions more instead of just being solid, more intense. When I needed that, I could play long rallies and be in defense, as well. Everything was working today."
